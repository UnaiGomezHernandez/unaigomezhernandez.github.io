I am a researcher and policy analyst specialising in EU external action, EU–China relations, geoeconomics, artificial intelligence, strategic communication, and policymaking.

I currently work as a researcher on AI and strategy for the European Commission and as Guest Professor at KU Leuven, focusing on EU–China relations.

I completed my joint PhD at the University of Edinburgh and KU Leuven in June 2026. My doctoral research examined the influence of radical right populist parties on the EU’s external action towards China in the European Parliament.
