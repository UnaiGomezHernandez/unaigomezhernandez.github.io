I am a researcher and policy analyst specialising in EU external action, EU–China relations, geoeconomics, artificial intelligence, strategic communication, and policymaking. I currently work as a researcher on AI and strategy for the European Commission and as Guest Professor at KU Leuven, focusing on EU–China relations.

My work connects EU external action and policymaking with debates on geopolitical change, technology, and political contestation. My research interests include EU–China relations, geoeconomics and economic security, artificial intelligence and AI governance, strategic communication, European public policy, illiberalism, and the future of the liberal international order. I combine quantitative and qualitative approaches, including computational social science, text-as-data analysis, traditional content analysis, elite interviews, and comparative research. My methodological experience also includes topic modelling, word-frequency analysis, qualitative comparative analysis, participant observation, and computational text analysis in R.

I completed my joint PhD at the University of Edinburgh and KU Leuven in June 2026. My doctoral research examined the influence of radical right populist parties on the EU’s external action towards China in the European Parliament. I also hold a voluntary position as an Early Career Researcher on EU–China relations with the University of Edinburgh and KU Leuven. In 2026, I was a Research Trainee on Communication, Strategy, and Artificial Intelligence at the European Commission’s DG COMM.

I previously worked as Assistant to the Delegate of the Basque Country to the European Union, as an EU environmental policy monitoring officer at Aclima, and as a researcher at the University of the Basque Country. At the University of Edinburgh, I taught seminars and tutorials in political science and international relations and delivered a workshop on analysing polarisation in political texts using R.

Before my joint PhD, I studied at the College of Europe, the London School of Economics and Political Science, the University of the Basque Country, and Aberystwyth University. I work in Spanish, English, French, and Basque, with beginner knowledge of Chinese and Russian.
